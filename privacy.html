PTPA privacy policy

PTPA is a personal app, used only by the person who made it, on their own computer.

What it accesses

With permission given through Google's sign-in, PTPA reads the list of calendars and the events in Google Calendar, and adds events. It removes only events it added itself.

What happens to the data

Calendar data is kept on the owner's own computer and used only to plan their days. Planning is done with an AI model, which may see event details as part of that. The data isn't sold, shared with anyone else or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's third-party connections page, at myaccount.google.com/connections.
